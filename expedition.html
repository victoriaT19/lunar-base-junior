🌙 Lunar Base Junior

Mira suits up and steps out of the airlock of Hopper Base for a surface expedition at the Shackleton crater rim. Explore, mine, scan discoveries and bring your haul home. A solar storm could hit at any moment.

Everything you carry home goes into the base: build with it, craft with it and feed your crew. Walk back to the lander and press E to return to base. Real moonwalks: on Apollo 17, two astronauts spent about 22 hours outside over three EVAs.

  • Life support drains as you breathe. Refill at the lander or refine oxygen.
  • Hazard protection shields you from cosmic radiation. Solar storms drain it fast. Polymer plates make it stronger.
  • Suit energy powers your mining beam. It recharges in sunlight and drains in the dark of the cold craters, where the ice is.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sprint, hold click or Space to mine, E to scan or use, I for the menu. On a phone: use the joystick and buttons.
